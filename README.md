# Olist E-commerce Product Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the performance of the Olist e-commerce platform using data on orders, customers, products and customer reviews.

The main objective is to identify key patterns in sales performance, customer experience, product categories and geographical distribution of orders.

## Business Questions

The analysis addresses the following questions:

* How did GMV and order volume change over time?
* How is Average Order Value (AOV) distributed over the observation period?
* Is there a relationship between order volume and GMV?
* How does delivery time relate to customer satisfaction?
* Which product categories generate the highest GMV?
* Which Brazilian states have the highest number of orders?

### Sales Performance

GMV generally increased during the main observation period, although the growth was not continuous.

The correlation between monthly GMV and the number of orders is **0.990**, indicating a very strong positive relationship between the two metrics.

Average Order Value fluctuates approximately between **125 and 150**, with several noticeable changes over time.

The final incomplete observation was excluded from the main trend analysis because it contained only one order and therefore did not represent a complete month.

### Customer Experience

Delivery time is strongly associated with customer satisfaction.

The average review score decreases from **4.48** for orders delivered in less than 3 days to **3.72** for orders delivered in 14 days or more.

The share of negative reviews increases from **6.64%** for the shortest delivery group to **22.59%** for orders delivered in 14 days or more.

### Product Categories

The highest GMV is generated by several major product categories.

The leading categories include:

* `beleza_saude`
* `relogios_presentes`
* `cama_mesa_banho`
* `esporte_lazer`
* `informatica_acessorios`

The `beleza_saude` category generated approximately **1.26 million** in GMV in the analyzed dataset.

### Geographical Distribution

Orders are geographically concentrated in several Brazilian states.

**São Paulo (SP)** is the largest market with **41,746 orders**, followed by Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and Minas Gerais (MG).

## Business Implications

The analysis suggests three potential areas for improvement:

1. **Reduce long delivery times**, particularly deliveries exceeding 14 days, to improve customer satisfaction.
2. **Maintain and increase order volume**, as it is strongly associated with total GMV.
3. **Focus commercial and marketing efforts on high-performing product categories and major geographical markets.**

These findings represent associations observed in the data and should not be interpreted as evidence of causal relationships.


## Dataset

The analysis uses the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

The dataset contains information about orders, customers, products, order items, payments and customer reviews.
